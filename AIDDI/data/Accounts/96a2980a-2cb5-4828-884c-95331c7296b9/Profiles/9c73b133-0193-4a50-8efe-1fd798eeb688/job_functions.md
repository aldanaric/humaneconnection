Math teacher
1. Lesson Planning & Instruction
Develop and deliver engaging lessons aligned with curriculum standards.
Explain mathematical concepts clearly using a variety of instructional strategies to meet diverse student needs.

2. Classroom Communication & Facilitation
Lead classroom discussions, provide verbal instruction, and encourage student participation.
Adjust explanations based on student understanding and questions.

3. Student Assessment & Feedback
Create quizzes, tests, and assignments to measure student learning.
Provide timely, constructive feedback that helps students improve mathematical reasoning.

4. Classroom Management & Student Support
Maintain a positive learning environment that encourages participation and respect.
Support students who require additional instruction or accommodations.

5. Collaboration & Professional Development
Work with fellow teachers to align curriculum and share instructional strategies.
Participate in department meetings, parent conferences, and professional learning opportunities.

6. Administrative Responsibilities
Maintain accurate grade records and attendance.
Communicate student progress with parents, counselors, and school administrators.
