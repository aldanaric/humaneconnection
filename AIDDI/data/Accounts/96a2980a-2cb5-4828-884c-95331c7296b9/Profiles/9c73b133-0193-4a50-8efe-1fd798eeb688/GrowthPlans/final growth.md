# 30-day Growth Plan Final

**Employee:** Leora Bell

**Role:** Janitor

**Type:** INTJ-A (Architect)

**Core Functions:** Performing cleaning, maintenance, and waste management tasks; ensuring facility cleanliness and safety; managing supplies; and reporting facility issues.

## 1. Purpose and Framework
This plan focuses on empowering Leora to strategically optimize janitorial processes, enhance her professional communication with colleagues, and strengthen her proactive ownership of facility cleanliness and maintenance.

It makes use of the three concepts of:
- **Humane Connection&trade;**: Presence, Empathy, Clarity, Adaptability, Recognition of Humanity
- **Owning Your Work**: Define, Choose, Zoom Out, Submit, Absorb, Teach.
- **Behavior-Change science**:
    - **Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg):** Breaking down desired behaviors into small, easy-to-start actions anchored to existing routines.
    - **Habit Loop (Charles Duhigg):** Understanding and redesigning the cue-routine-reward cycle to establish new, desired behaviors.

## 2. Development Priorities

### 1. Strategic Process Improvement
**Behavioral goal:** Leora consistently identifies and proposes 1-2 practical improvements to janitorial processes or facility maintenance each week.
**Humane Connection Link:** Clarity in her proposals ensures her insights are understood, and recognition of her humanity validates her valuable analytical contributions, fostering trust and engagement.
**Method:** Leora will apply a "Think-Fix-Report" framework, leveraging her innate "continuous improvement" and "innovative mindset." She will observe current processes or recurring issues, identify a potential improvement or solution, and document it concisely for review. This capitalizes on her "objective judgment" and "self-directed learning."
**Supervisor Role:** Provide clear channels for Leora to submit her suggestions, offer positive feedback on her analytical contributions, and engage in discussions about the feasibility and impact of her proposed improvements.

### 2. Effective Professional Communication
**Behavioral goal:** Leora proactively initiates brief, positive professional interactions with at least one colleague daily and delivers constructive observations with empathetic framing.
**Humane Connection Link:** Empathy allows her to tailor her communication, and clarity ensures her message is received as intended, fostering better collaboration and psychological safety within the team.
**Method:** Leora will implement a "Greeting & Glimpse" routine. Before starting a task in a common area, she will offer a brief, positive greeting to a nearby colleague. When reporting an observation (e.g., a maintenance issue), she will practice framing it neutrally or with a solution-oriented tone, acknowledging potential impact on others. This uses BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits by anchoring a greeting to entering a common space.
**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Observing a situation that needs reporting OR encountering a colleague in a common space.
- Routine: (Old: Directly stating the issue bluntly / avoiding interaction). (New: Pause, consider the other person's perspective / offer a brief, positive greeting).
- Reward: (New: Positive recognition from colleagues, feeling of effective and respectful contribution, improved working relationships).
**Supervisor Role:** Model empathetic and clear communication, provide specific, objective feedback on Leora's communication style, and create low-pressure opportunities for positive team interaction.

### 3. Proactive Ownership and Initiative
**Behavioral goal:** Leora independently identifies and addresses minor facility needs or reports significant issues with recommended solutions before being assigned.
**Humane Connection Link:** Her adaptability in problem-solving demonstrates her initiative, and recognizing her humanity validates her autonomy and contributions beyond the checklist, driving intrinsic motivation.
**Method:** Leora will utilize a "See-Decide-Act/Report" framework. She will consciously "Zoom Out" from her immediate tasks to observe the broader environment, "Define" emerging needs, and "Choose" to either resolve minor issues herself or "Submit" a clear report with a proposed solution for larger concerns. This directly applies the "Owning Your Work" principles of Define, Choose, Zoom Out, and Submit, leveraging her "conceptual thinking" and "autonomy."
**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Completing a standard routine janitorial task.
- Routine: (Old: Moving directly to the next assigned task). (New: Take 2 minutes to "Zoom Out" and scan the surrounding area for unassigned needs or potential improvements).
- Reward: (New: Sense of accomplishment, feeling of effective contribution, reduced personal frustration with inefficiency, increased sense of control
