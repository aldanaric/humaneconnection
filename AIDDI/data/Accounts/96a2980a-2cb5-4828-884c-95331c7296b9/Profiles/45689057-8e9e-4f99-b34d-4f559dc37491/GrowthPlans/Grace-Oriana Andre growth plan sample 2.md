# 30-day Growth Plan

**Employee:** Grace-Oriana Andre

**Role:** Impact Investment Analyst

**Type:** INTJ-A (Architect)

**Core Functions:** Grace-Oriana analyzes portfolio investments using financial and impact metrics, researches economic landscapes, builds financial models, and monitors performance. She also documents activities, communicates findings, collaborates with teams on initiatives, builds external relationships, and supports endowment management and mission alignment.

## 1. Purpose and Framework
This plan will focus on empowering Grace-Oriana to proactively seek clarity, confidently navigate uncertainty, and enhance her collaborative communication to improve task accuracy and team efficiency.

It makes use of the three concepts of:
- **Humane Connection&trade;**: Presence, Empathy, Clarity, Adaptability, Recognition of Humanity
- **Owning Your Work**: Define, Choose, Zoom Out, Submit, Absorb, Teach.
- **Behavior-Change science**:
    - **Charles Duhigg's Habit Loop**: Focuses on identifying cues, routines, and rewards to understand and change existing behaviors.
    - **Carol Dweck's Growth Mindset**: Emphasizes the belief that abilities can be developed through dedication and hard work, fostering a love of learning and resilience.

## 2. Development Priorities

**1. Proactive Inquiry & Clarification**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace-Oriana will consistently ask at least one clarifying question when receiving new tasks or encountering initial ambiguity, before proceeding with work.

**Humane Connection Link:** This links to **Clarity** by ensuring shared understanding and minimizing assumptions. It also leverages **Presence** as the supervisor actively listens and responds to her inquiries, fostering psychological safety.

**Method:** Grace-Oriana will implement "habit stacking" (James Clear) by associating asking a clarifying question with the immediate conclusion of a task assignment or the moment she identifies an initial point of confusion. The supervisor will use "frequent small wins" (Teresa Amabile) by praising any inquiry, no matter how small, to build confidence.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Supervisor assigns a new task or Grace-Oriana identifies initial uncertainty.
- Routine (old): Grace-Oriana responds "no questions," then struggles or makes errors.
- Reward (new): Immediate clarity, prevention of rework, supervisor's explicit positive reinforcement, and increased confidence in understanding the task.

**Supervisor Role:** Proactively invite questions, explicitly state that all questions are valued, model asking questions, and provide immediate, positive feedback for any inquiry.

**2. Navigating Uncertainty & Seeking Support**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace-Oriana will articulate specific areas of uncertainty or blockage on analytical tasks and request support or resources within 24 hours of encountering them.

**Humane Connection Link:** This connects to **Empathy** as the supervisor understands Grace-Oriana's discomfort with ambiguity (a noted weakness for INTJ-As) and **Recognition of Humanity** by validating her need for support as part of the learning process. **Adaptability** comes into play as the supervisor adjusts support based on Grace-Oriana's specific needs.

**Method:** Grace-Oriana will leverage "implementation intentions" (Peter Gollwitzer) by pre-determining *when* and *how* she will seek help (e.g., "If I am stuck for more than 30 minutes on an analytical task, then I will email my supervisor with a specific question or call a colleague"). The supervisor will foster a "Growth Mindset" (Carol Dweck) by reframing uncertainty as a learning opportunity rather than a failure, encouraging experimentation and iteration.

**Supervisor Role:** Normalize asking for help, provide specific channels for support (e.g., dedicated check-in slots, preferred communication methods), offer concrete examples of problem-solving strategies, and share personal examples of navigating uncertainty.

**3. Collaborative Communication & Team Integration**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace-Oriana will actively contribute to team discussions by offering insights or asking questions and will proactively seek peer feedback on her analytical outputs at least once per week.

**Humane Connection Link:** This relates to **Presence** by encouraging Grace-Oriana to be an active participant in team interactions and **Clarity** through contributing her unique analytical perspective. It builds **Recognition of Humanity** by valuing diverse contributions and fostering shared ownership.

**Method:** Grace-Oriana will utilize "social learning theory" (Albert Bandura) by observing and identifying effective communication and collaboration patterns within the team. The supervisor will create structured opportunities for peer feedback (e.g., brief 'desk-side reviews' or 'analytical sparring sessions') to encourage "reciprocal altruism" and make seeking input a routine part of the workflow.

**Supervisor Role:** Facilitate inclusive team discussions, assign Grace-Oriana to small, collaborative analytical tasks, provide specific feedback on her communication style, and highlight the value of her objective judgment in team settings.

## Conclusion
This plan will help Grace-Oriana leverage her INTJ-A strengths in strategic thinking and independent work while developing crucial interpersonal skills for effective collaboration. By focusing on proactive inquiry, navigating uncertainty, and integrated communication, she will enhance her performance, reduce rework, and become a more engaged and impactful team member.

### How Performance Thrives Through Humane Connection&trade;
- **Presence builds psychological safety** When managers listen actively and  respond thoughtfully, employees feel seen and heard. This reduces fear-
  based shutdowns and encourages real-time inquiry, which improves task
  clarity and execution.
- **Empathy fosters resilience and engagement** Stepping into an employee’s
  perspective helps managers tailor support and recognize effort, not just
  outcomes. This strengthens trust and motivates sustained effort, especially
  during complex or ambiguous tasks.
- **Recognition of Humanity deepens accountability** When people feel their
  worth is acknowledged, they’re more likely to take ownership of their work.
  This expression reinforces dignity, which drives intrinsic motivation and pride
  in contribution.
- **Clarity reduces rework and accelerates progress** Clear, candid, and kind
  communication ensures expectations are understood. It minimizes
  confusion, enables better planning, and allows employees to self-correct
  before errors compound.
- **Adaptability increases effectiveness across differences** Adjusting tone
  and delivery to meet individual contexts helps managers connect across
  styles and stress responses. This flexibility improves coaching outcomes and
  team cohesion.

### Why Behavior-Change Science Amplifies These Effects
- **It transforms insight into habit** Humane Connection creates the conditions
  for trust and reflection. Behavior-change science ensures those insights are
  translated into repeatable actions through cues, reinforcement, and
  feedback loops.
- **It targets the root, not just the symptom** Instead of reacting to
  performance gaps, behavior-change frameworks help managers identify
  triggers, beliefs, and environmental factors that shape behavior, leading to
  more sustainable growth.
- **It builds adaptive capacity** By combining emotional resonance with
  behavioral modeling, managers help employees navigate uncertainty, build
  new skills, and internalize ownership.
