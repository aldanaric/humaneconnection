# 30-day Growth Plan

**Employee:** Grace-Oriana Andre

**Role:** Impact Investment Analyst

**Type:** INTJ-A (Architect)

**Core Functions:** Analyze portfolio investments (financial/impact); build and maintain financial models; create internal/external communications; collaborate on organizational initiatives; build external relationships; assist with endowment management and mission alignment.

## 1. Purpose and Framework
This plan will focus on enhancing Grace-Oriana’s ability to proactively seek clarification, manage emotional responses to uncertainty, and communicate collaboratively.

It makes use of the three concepts of:
- **Humane Connection&trade;**:Presence, Empathy, Clarity, Adaptability, Recognition of Humanity
- **Owning Your Work**:Define, Choose, Zoom Out, Submit, Absorb, Teach.
- **Behavior-Change science**:
    - **Habit Loop (Charles Duhigg):** Explains how habits are formed through cues, routines, and rewards, providing a framework for intentional behavior change.
    - **Self-Determination Theory (Edward Deci & Richard Ryan):** Focuses on intrinsic motivation, emphasizing the importance of autonomy, competence, and relatedness for fostering sustained growth.

## 2. Development Priorities

### Development Priority 1: Proactive Clarification and Inquiry
**Behavioral goal:** Grace-Oriana will consistently ask 1-2 clarifying questions for new tasks or when encountering analytical ambiguity.
**Humane Connection Link:** Clarity reduces rework and accelerates progress by ensuring expectations are understood, while Presence builds psychological safety, encouraging inquiry.
**Method:** Grace-Oriana will utilize the "Define" and "Choose" stages of Owning Your Work to break down ambiguous tasks and formulate specific questions. She will apply the Habit Loop by creating a routine of inquiry, linking task assignment (cue) to question formulation (routine) and improved understanding (reward).
**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Receiving a new task or encountering an analytical problem where clarity is needed.
- Routine: Pause, jot down 1-2 specific questions, then ask the supervisor or a peer.
- Reward: Gaining immediate clarity, faster task completion, increased sense of competence and control (aligning with Self-Determination Theory for INTJs who value "what works").
**Supervisor Role:** Model asking clarifying questions, create intentional pauses after task assignments for questions, and praise specific, well-articulated inquiries.

### Development Priority 2: Emotional Regulation and Seeking Support
**Behavioral goal:** Grace-Oriana will identify and articulate moments of uncertainty or overwhelm, and proactively communicate a need for support within 24 hours.
**Humane Connection Link:** Empathy fosters resilience by supporting Grace-Oriana through challenges, and Recognition of Humanity deepens accountability by validating her experience while encouraging ownership.
**Method:** Grace-Oriana will practice the "Absorb" stage of Owning Your Work by reflecting on her emotional responses to uncertainty. She will connect seeking support to her intrinsic motivation for competence (Self-Determination Theory), understanding that asking for help is a strategic choice for mastering complex analytical tasks and achieving her perfectionistic standards more effectively.
**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Feeling overwhelmed, stuck, or experiencing "freezing" when facing ambiguity.
- Routine: Take a mindful pause (e.g., 60 seconds), acknowledge the feeling, identify the specific source of discomfort, and formulate one clear request for guidance or a thought-out question.
- Reward: Reduced stress, shared problem-solving, improved progress, enhanced self-awareness, and stronger team relatedness.
**Supervisor Role:** Normalize uncertainty and challenges, explicitly state that asking for help is a strength, and offer structured, low-stakes opportunities for check-ins where challenges can be shared.

### Development Priority 3: Collaborative Communication and Empathetic Feedback
**Behavioral goal:** Grace-Oriana will actively participate in collaborative discussions, offering feedback framed with an understanding of others' perspectives, and seeking input from peers.
**Humane Connection Link:** Adaptability increases effectiveness by allowing Grace-Oriana to adjust her communication style, while Clarity ensures her innovative ideas are understood and adopted.
**Method:** Grace-Oriana will utilize the "Teach" stage of Owning Your Work by explaining her strategic insights to others, which necessitates adapting her communication style. She will integrate the Habit Loop into her feedback process, pausing (cue) to consider the recipient's perspective and shared goals (routine) before delivering her logical insights (reward: more effective collaboration). For an INTJ-A, this fosters relatedness and competence through more effective team outcomes.
**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Preparing to give feedback or participating in a team discussion about process improvement.
- Routine: Before speaking, mentally frame feedback around shared goals, consider the impact on others, and specifically ask for others' perspectives.
- Reward: Positive team responses, better buy-in for solutions, improved team efficiency, and stronger collaborative relationships.
**Supervisor Role:** Model empathetic and collaborative communication, provide specific scenarios for Grace-Oriana to practice, and create structured forums for cross-functional input.

## Conclusion

### How Performance Thrives Through Humane Connection&trade;
- **Presence builds psychological safety** When managers listen actively and
  respond thoughtfully, employees feel seen and heard. This reduces fear-
  based shutdowns and encourages real-time inquiry, which improves task
  clarity and execution.
- **Empathy fosters resilience and engagement** Stepping into an employee’s
  perspective helps managers tailor support and recognize effort, not just
  outcomes. This strengthens trust and motivates sustained effort, especially
  during complex or ambiguous tasks.
- **Recognition of Humanity deepens accountability** When people feel their
  worth is acknowledged, they’re more likely to take ownership of their work.
  This expression reinforces dignity, which drives intrinsic motivation and pride
  in contribution.
- **Clarity reduces rework and accelerates progress** Clear, candid, and kind
  communication ensures expectations are understood. It minimizes
  confusion, enables better planning, and allows employees to self-correct
  before errors compound.
- **Adaptability increases effectiveness across differences** Adjusting tone
  and delivery to meet individual contexts helps managers connect across
  styles and stress responses. This flexibility improves coaching outcomes and
  team cohesion.

### Why Behavior-Change Science Amplifies These Effects
- **It transforms insight into habit** Humane Connection creates the conditions
  for trust and reflection. Behavior-change science ensures those insights are
  translated into repeatable actions through cues, reinforcement, and
  feedback loops.
- **It targets the root, not just the symptom** Instead of reacting to
  performance gaps, behavior-change frameworks help managers identify
  triggers, beliefs, and environmental factors that shape behavior, leading to
  more sustainable growth.
- **It builds adaptive capacity** By combining emotional resonance with
  behavioral modeling, managers help employees navigate uncertainty, build
  new skills, and internalize ownership.
