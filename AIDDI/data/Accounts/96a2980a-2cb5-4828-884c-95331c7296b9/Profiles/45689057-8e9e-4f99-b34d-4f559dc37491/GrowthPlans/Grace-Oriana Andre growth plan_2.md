# 30-day Growth Plan

**Employee:** Grace-Oriana Andre

**Role:** Impact Investment Analyst

**Type:** INTJ-A (Architect)

**Core Functions:** Analyze portfolio investments, build financial models, create internal/external communications, collaborate on initiatives, build external relationships, and support endowment management.

## 1. Purpose and Framework
This plan focuses on empowering Grace-Oriana to proactively seek clarity, confidently navigate ambiguity, and effectively leverage her analytical strengths through collaborative communication.

It makes use of the three concepts of:
- **Humane Connection&trade;**:Presence, Empathy, Clarity, Adaptability, Recognition of Humanity
- **Owning Your Work**:Define, Choose, Zoom Out, Submit, Absorb, Teach.
- **Behavior-Change science**:
    - **BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits**: Focuses on starting small, building momentum, and making new desired behaviors easy to adopt by linking them to existing routines.
    - **Carol Dweck's Growth Mindset**: Cultivates a belief that abilities can be developed through dedication and hard work, reframing challenges as opportunities for learning and growth.

## 2. Development Priorities

**1. Proactive Clarification and Inquiry**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace will consistently ask clarifying questions to ensure task understanding, reduce rework, and improve task execution.

**Humane Connection Link:** **Clarity** is achieved through open dialogue, ensuring expectations are understood. **Presence** from the supervisor encourages real-time inquiry, building psychological safety for Grace to ask.

**Method:** Grounded in **BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits**, Grace will identify a small, specific "anchor" behavior (e.g., after every task assignment or meeting conclusion) to prompt her to ask at least one specific clarifying question. This lowers the bar for initiating inquiry. The supervisor will then provide immediate, positive reinforcement for any questions asked, focusing on the act of asking, not just the content.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Supervisor assigns a task or concludes a discussion.
- Routine: Ask one specific clarifying question (e.g., "What does success look like for this output?" or "What's the immediate next step here?").
- Reward: Supervisor provides immediate, positive verbal acknowledgment (e.g., "Great question, Grace. That helps me ensure clarity.")

**Supervisor Role:** Explicitly invite questions after assigning tasks, model asking clarifying questions, and provide non-judgmental, encouraging responses to any question Grace-Oriana asks.

---

**2. Embracing Learning through Proactive Support-Seeking**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace will actively seek and accept support or guidance when encountering ambiguity, uncertainty, or complex analytical challenges, reframing these moments as learning opportunities.

**Humane Connection Link:** **Empathy** helps the supervisor understand Grace-Oriana's discomfort with uncertainty. **Recognition of Humanity** acknowledges that seeking help is a natural part of growth, fostering trust.

**Method:** Leveraging **Carol Dweck's Growth Mindset**, Grace-Oriana will be encouraged to view moments of confusion as opportunities to expand her knowledge rather than as failures. The supervisor will normalize uncertainty, share examples of their own learning challenges, and establish a "think-aloud" protocol where Grace-Oriana articulates her thought process (even when uncertain) as a preliminary step before asking for help.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Encountering an ambiguous task or analytical roadblock.
- Routine: Engage in a "think-aloud" to herself, identifying where the uncertainty lies, then message supervisor with "I'm encountering X, and my initial thought is Y, but I'm unsure about Z. Can we connect briefly?"
- Reward: Timely, supportive response and collaborative problem-solving from the supervisor, reinforcing that seeking help leads to progress and shared understanding.

**Supervisor Role:** Create a safe environment for vulnerability, proactively schedule brief "check-in" opportunities for specific analytical tasks, and frame challenges as collective learning experiences.

---

**3. Enhancing Collaborative Application of Analytical Skills**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace will actively apply her analytical strengths within team discussions and projects by openly sharing her thought process and inviting early feedback.

**Humane Connection Link:** **Adaptability** in communication ensures Grace-Oriana can adjust her directness for team dynamics. **Recognition of Humanity** validates her intellectual contributions and helps build team trust.

**Method:** Combining **BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits** with **Growth Mindset** principles, Grace-Oriana will commit to sharing a "draft" of her analytical thinking or initial findings in a team setting (e.g., 5-minute update) before perfection. This shifts the focus from delivering a flawless final product to engaging in iterative, collaborative development. The supervisor will facilitate specific opportunities for her to present "work in progress" and guide the team in providing constructive feedback.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
- Cue: Beginning an analytical task or preparing for a team update.
- Routine: Identify one specific point of analysis or a hypothesis to share with the team, framing it as "initial thoughts for feedback."
- Reward: Team engagement, constructive discussion, and supervisor affirmation of the value of her insights and early contributions.

**Supervisor Role:** Structure team meetings to include "work-in-progress" sharing, specifically invite Grace-Oriana's analytical input, and coach the team on providing supportive, constructive feedback.
