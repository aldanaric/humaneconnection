Top Functions of Impact Investment Analyst 
1. Research & Analysis
Analyze portfolio investments using both financial and impact metrics.
Research economic, market, and political landscapes, especially their effects on underinvested communities.
2. Financial Modeling & Performance Monitoring
Build and maintain financial models to track portfolio performance.
Connect financial outcomes to mission impact for beneficiaries.
3. Documentation & Communication
Create internal and external materials that explain investment activities and mission‐aligned initiatives.
Support the Director of Investments (DoI) in preparing reports and presentations for the Board, Trustees, and external stakeholders.
4. Collaboration & Team Engagement
Work with the broader GMF team on organizational communications and programmatic initiatives.
Contribute to cross‐team projects that align investments with the Fund’s mission.
5. Relationship Building & External Engagement
Build relationships with peers in impact investing and philanthropy.
Participate in mission‐aligned programs and projects with external partners.
6. Portfolio & Endowment Support
Assist the DoI in managing the $140M endowment.
Support activities like investor activism, community investment development, and tracking mission alignment metrics.
