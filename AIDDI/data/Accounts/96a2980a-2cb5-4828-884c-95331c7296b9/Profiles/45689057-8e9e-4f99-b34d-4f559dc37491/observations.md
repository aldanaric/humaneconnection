## Area
        Lack of Clarifying Questions

        ### Observation
        When tasks are assigned, Grace consistently responds that she has no questions— even when prompted multiple times. However, the resulting work is often incomplete, incorrect, or not submitted at all. 

        ### Impact
        This pattern makes it difficult for the supervisor to assess Grace’s understanding and provide targeted support. Without questions, the supervisor cannot identify where confusion exists, which delays progress and increases rework. 
        
## Area
        Emotional Response to Uncertainty

        ### Observation
        In moments of uncertainty, Grace appears to freeze or shut down. The supervisor suspects this may be a fear-based response—akin to a “deer in headlights”—where the discomfort of not knowing prevents her from asking for help. 

        ### Impact
        This reaction creates a communication gap. It prevents real-time course correction and leads to repeated errors, which affects the supervisor’s ability to train effectively and strains team timelines
        
## Area
        Difficulty with Analytical Tasks

        ### Observation
        Grace is struggling to grasp the more analytical aspects of her role. The supervisor is willing to provide training but notes that Grace’s lack of inquiry makes it hard to tailor that support.

        ### Impact
        Without insight into what Grace understands or where she’s stuck, the supervisor must guess or wait until errors surface. This reactive approach slows team momentum and contributes to frustration when tasks must be redone.
        
## Area
        Team-level Consequences

        ### Observation
        The supervisor has expressed that Grace’s current approach—particularly the absence of clarifying questions—hinders the team’s ability to move efficiently and collaboratively.

        ### Impact
        Repeated task revisions, unclear understanding, and missed opportunities for coaching affect not just Grace’s development but the broader team’s workflow, morale, and trust in handoffs.
