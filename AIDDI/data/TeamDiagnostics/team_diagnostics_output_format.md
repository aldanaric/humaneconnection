# Team Diagnostics Output Format
Generate a professional Team Diagnostics report using the following structure.

---

# Executive Summary
Provide a concise overview of the team's overall dynamics.

Summarize:
- Primary team strengths
- Primary challenges
- Overall communication style
- Leadership observations
- Overall collaboration health

Keep this section to 2–4 paragraphs.

---

# Team Personality Overview
Summarize the overall personality composition of the team.

Include observations such as:
- Personality distribution
- Introvert vs. Extravert balance
- Thinking vs. Feeling balance
- Judging vs. Prospecting balance
- Assertive vs. Turbulent balance

Describe how these patterns influence team behavior.

---

# Communication Dynamics
Describe:

- How the team naturally communicates
- Communication strengths
- Potential communication gaps
- Areas where misunderstandings may occur
- Recommendations for improving communication

---

# Collaboration Strengths
Identify the team's strongest collaborative qualities.

Highlight:
- Complementary personalities
- Shared strengths
- Innovation
- Leadership
- Trust-building behaviors

---

# Potential Friction Points
Identify potential areas of friction.

Examples include:
- Decision-making
- Conflict styles
- Feedback preferences
- Stress responses
- Work pace
- Delegation
- Communication differences

Avoid assigning blame.

Describe behaviors objectively and constructively.

---

# Team Growth Opportunities
Provide practical recommendations that improve:
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Psychological safety
- Accountability
- Leadership
- Conflict management

Recommendations should be realistic and actionable.

---

# Pairwise Collaboration Insights

For each pair of team members provide:

## Complementary Strengths

## Potential Friction

## Recommended Communication Strategies

## Suggested Discussion Questions

---

# Leadership Insights
Provide observations specifically for organizational leadership.

Include:
- Hidden team strengths
- Organizational risks
- Coaching opportunities
- Leadership blind spots
- Opportunities to improve alignment

---

# Action Plan
Provide a prioritized action plan.

Include:
## Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)

## Mid-Term Actions (30–90 Days)

## Long-Term Development Opportunities

---

# Closing Summary

Conclude with a positive summary emphasizing the team's potential, strengths, and opportunities for continued growth through the Humane Connection™ framework.
