# 30-day Growth Plan

**Employee:** Grace Andre

**Role:** Impact Investment Analyst

**Type:** INTJ-A (Architect)

**Core Functions:** Analyzing investments, financial modeling, creating reports, collaborating, building relationships, and supporting endowment management, requiring strong analytical, strategic, and communication skills.

## 1. Purpose and Framework
This plan will focus on empowering Grace to proactively seek clarity, confidently navigate uncertainty, and take full ownership of her work outcomes.

It makes use of the three concepts of:
-   **Humane Connection&trade;**: Presence, Empathy, Clarity, Adaptability, Recognition of Humanity
-   **Owning Your Work**: Define, Choose, Zoom Out, Submit, Absorb, Teach.
-   **Behavior-Change science**:
    -   **Self-Efficacy Theory (Bandura):** The belief in one's capacity to execute behaviors necessary to produce specific performance attainments. This is crucial for Grace to overcome her fear-based responses and believe in her ability to ask questions and master analytical tasks.
    -   **Habit Loop (Duhigg):** Explains how habits are formed and sustained through a Cue, Routine, and Reward cycle. This framework will be used to design new behaviors for proactive inquiry and seeking support.

## 2. Development Priorities

**1. Proactive Inquiry and Communication Clarity**
**Behavioral goal:** Grace will consistently ask clarifying questions to ensure full understanding of tasks and expectations before beginning work.
**Humane Connection Link:** Clarity reduces rework and accelerates progress by ensuring expectations are understood, which enables better planning and allows Grace to self-correct before errors compound.
**Method:** Grace will utilize a structured inquiry template (e.g., covering scope, deliverables, stakeholders, success metrics) to prepare specific questions for every new task assignment. This method leverages her INTJ preference for logic, structure, and thoroughness.
**Behavior-Change Design:**
-   **Cue:** Supervisor assigns a new task or project.
-   **Routine:** Grace pauses, reviews the task, and populates her structured inquiry template with specific questions.
-   **Reward:** Gaining immediate clarity, reducing the likelihood of rework, receiving positive feedback on her proactivity, and experiencing a sense of competence and control over the task, thereby building Self-Efficacy.
**Supervisor Role:** Explicitly state the expectation for Grace to use the inquiry template. Allocate dedicated time post-assignment for Grace to present her questions. Provide positive reinforcement for clear and thoughtful inquiries.

**2. Navigating Ambiguity and Seeking Support**
**Behavioral goal:** Grace will actively seek support or resources within 24 hours of encountering uncertainty or roadblocks on a task.
**Humane Connection Link:** Presence builds psychological safety by making Grace feel seen and heard when she raises concerns, while Empathy fosters her resilience by validating her challenges and offering tailored support.
**Method:** Grace will implement "if-then" planning (Gollwitzer) for moments of uncertainty: "If I feel stuck or uncertain on a task for more than 30 minutes, then I will identify the specific point of confusion and draft a concise message or email to my supervisor outlining the problem and any potential solutions I've considered." This addresses her discomfort with ambiguity and hesitancy to seek support.
**Behavior-Change Design:**
-   **Cue:** Experiencing a feeling of being stuck, confused, or "freezing" on a task for a defined period.
-   **Routine:** Identify the precise barrier, formulate a clear, objective request for guidance or resources.
-   **Reward:** Overcoming obstacles, making tangible progress, reducing personal stress, receiving validation and collaborative support, reinforcing her Self-Efficacy in problem-solving.
**Supervisor Role:** Normalize seeking help by sharing instances where asking for support led to better outcomes. Provide specific, low-barrier channels for Grace to ask questions (e.g., a short daily check-in, specific Slack channel). Actively acknowledge and praise Grace's efforts to seek assistance and engage in problem-solving.

**3. Comprehensive Task Ownership**
**Behavioral goal:** Grace will proactively define and confirm the scope, requirements, and success metrics for each task, ensuring complete submission or clear communication of status.
**Humane Connection Link:** Recognition of Humanity deepens accountability. When Grace feels her worth is acknowledged, she is more likely to take ownership of her work, fostering intrinsic motivation and pride in contribution. This aligns with the "Define" and "Submit" stages of Owning Your Work.
**Method:** Grace will adopt a "task definition checklist" for every new assignment, which she will complete and briefly review with her supervisor. This checklist will cover: the ultimate goal, key deliverables, quality standards, deadline, and necessary approvals. Upon completion or encountering a significant change, she will proactively communicate the status, leveraging her analytical and goal-oriented strengths.
**Behavior-Change Design:**
-   **Cue:** New task assigned or project initiated.
-   **Routine:** Complete the "task definition checklist," mentally confirm alignment with project goals, and communicate initial understanding to supervisor.
-   **Reward:** Delivering complete and correct work, receiving positive feedback on her thoroughness and ownership, experiencing a strong sense of accomplishment, and contributing effectively to team goals.
**Supervisor Role:** Regularly engage in a brief review of Grace's task definition checklist. Empower Grace to drive these clarification conversations. Provide specific, constructive feedback regarding the completeness and quality of her task submissions and status updates.

## Conclusion
This 30-day plan provides Grace with structured tools and a supportive environment to transform her approach to inquiry, ambiguity, and task ownership. By leveraging her INTJ strengths for logic and planning, while directly addressing her challenges in seeking support, Grace will enhance her clarity, reduce rework, and significantly boost her confidence and contribution to the team.

### How Performance Thrives Through Humane Connection&trade;
-   **Presence builds psychological safety** When managers listen actively and respond thoughtfully, employees feel seen and heard. This reduces fear-based shutdowns and encourages real-time inquiry, which improves task clarity and execution.
-   **Empathy fosters resilience and engagement** Stepping into an employee’s perspective helps managers tailor support and recognize effort, not just outcomes. This strengthens trust and motivates sustained effort, especially during complex or ambiguous tasks.
-   **Recognition of Humanity deepens accountability** When people feel their worth is acknowledged, they’re more likely to take ownership of their work. This expression reinforces dignity, which drives intrinsic motivation and pride in contribution.
-   **Clarity reduces rework and accelerates progress** Clear, candid, and kind communication ensures expectations are understood. It minimizes confusion, enables better planning, and allows employees to self-correct before errors compound.
-   **Adaptability increases effectiveness across differences** Adjusting tone and delivery to meet individual contexts helps managers connect across styles and stress responses. This flexibility improves coaching outcomes and team cohesion.

### Why Behavior-Change Science Amplifies These Effects
-   **It transforms insight into habit** Humane Connection creates the conditions for trust and reflection. Behavior-change science ensures those insights are translated into repeatable actions through cues, reinforcement, and feedback loops.
-   **It targets the root, not just the symptom** Instead of reacting to performance gaps, behavior-change frameworks help managers identify triggers, beliefs, and environmental factors that shape behavior, leading to more sustainable growth.
-   **It builds adaptive capacity** By combining emotional resonance with behavioral modeling, managers help employees navigate uncertainty, build new skills, and internalize ownership.
