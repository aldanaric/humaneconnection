# 30-day Growth Plan

**Employee:** Grace Andre

**Role:** Impact Investment Analyst

**Type:** INTJ-A (Architect)

**Core Functions:** Analyze portfolio investments, build financial models, create communication materials, collaborate on initiatives, build external relationships, and support endowment management.

## 1. Purpose and Framework
This plan will focus on empowering Grace to proactively seek clarity, effectively own her work from inception to completion, and enhance her collaborative communication to improve team efficiency and trust.

It makes use of the three concepts of:
-   **Humane Connection&trade;**: Presence, Empathy, Clarity, Adaptability, Recognition of Humanity
-   **Owning Your Work**: Define, Choose, Zoom Out, Submit, Absorb, Teach.
-   **Behavior-Change science**:
    *   **BJ Fogg's Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP)**: Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt converge. This helps design for Grace's "freezing" by reducing the ability barrier and adding clear prompts for action.
    *   **Charles Duhigg's Habit Loop (Cue-Routine-Reward)**: Habits are formed by cues triggering routines that lead to rewards. This will be used to replace less effective routines with new, productive ones.

## 2. Development Priorities

**Development Priority 1: Proactive Inquiry and Clarification**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace will consistently ask clarifying questions or verbalize areas of uncertainty *during* task assignment and initial work phases.

**Humane Connection Link:** Fosters Clarity by ensuring mutual understanding, demonstrates Presence through active engagement, and enables Adaptability when facing ambiguity.

**Method:** Leverage Fogg's B=MAP by reducing the "ability" barrier to asking questions (e.g., providing question templates) and adding "prompts" for inquiry. Use Duhigg's Habit Loop to replace the old routine of silence/freezing with a new routine of proactive questioning.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
-   **Cue:** New task assigned / Feeling of uncertainty arising.
-   **Routine (Old):** Remaining silent / Internalizing confusion / Freezing up.
-   **Routine (New):** Acknowledge uncertainty, verbalize it, ask specific questions, or propose an initial approach for feedback.
-   **Reward:** Gaining clarity, avoiding rework, feeling competent, receiving supervisor support.

**Supervisor Role:** Explicitly invite questions, normalize uncertainty as a learning tool, provide question prompts, and actively listen.

---

**Development Priority 2: Structured Task Ownership and Follow-Through**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace will consistently define tasks, plan execution, and provide clear progress updates or flag blockers before submission deadlines.

**Humane Connection Link:** Reinforces Recognition of Humanity through her contribution, enhances team Empathy by supporting workflow, and builds trust through Clarity in updates.

**Method:** Apply the "Owning Your Work" framework by breaking down "ownership" into smaller, prompted steps (Define, Choose, Zoom Out). Utilize Fogg's B=MAP by ensuring Grace has the "ability" (e.g., mini-planning templates) and "prompts" (e.g., scheduled check-ins) for consistent action.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
-   **Cue:** Task assigned with a deadline.
-   **Routine (Old):** Diving in without a clear plan / Getting stuck silently / Delayed or incomplete work.
-   **Routine (New):** Define scope and key steps, create a mini-plan, schedule internal check-ins, send brief progress notes.
-   **Reward:** Timely completion, reduced stress, supervisor trust, seeing concrete impact.

**Supervisor Role:** Help Grace define task scope, encourage mini-milestones, provide early feedback on her plans, and praise on-time updates.

---

**Development Priority 3: Collaborative Communication and Feedback Integration**

**Behavioral goal:** Grace will actively seek and constructively integrate feedback from peers and her supervisor, demonstrating openness to diverse perspectives.

**Humane Connection Link:** Fosters Empathy by understanding others’ inputs, promotes Adaptability to different communication styles, and strengthens Presence through active listening.

**Method:** Leverage Grace’s INTJ "Openness to Challenging Ideas." Frame feedback as objective "data" for continuous improvement (appealing to her logical nature). Use Fogg's B=MAP by creating clear "prompts" for feedback requests and Duhigg's Habit Loop to replace a potentially defensive routine with one of active integration.

**Behavior-Change Design:**
-   **Cue:** Project milestone / Draft submission / Feedback session.
-   **Routine (Old):** Internalizing critique / Focusing on perceived flaws / Potentially withdrawing.
-   **Routine (New):** Listen actively, ask clarifying questions about feedback, propose adjustments, or implement changes.
-   **Reward:** Enhanced project quality, stronger team relationships, feeling valued, improved system efficiency.

**Supervisor Role:** Structure feedback calmly and logically, highlight team benefits, provide specific examples, and model vulnerability when appropriate.

## Conclusion
This 30-day growth plan, tailored to Grace's INTJ strengths and development areas, will foster her ability to proactively seek clarity, confidently own her work, and engage more collaboratively. By integrating Humane Connection principles and behavior-change science, Grace will develop sustainable habits that enhance her performance and contribution to the team.

### How Performance Thrives Through Humane Connection&trade;
-   Presence builds psychological safety When managers listen actively and respond thoughtfully, employees feel seen and heard. This reduces fear-based shutdowns and encourages real-time inquiry, which improves task clarity and execution.
-   Empathy fosters resilience and engagement Stepping into an employee’s perspective helps managers tailor support and recognize effort, not just outcomes. This strengthens trust and motivates sustained effort, especially during complex or ambiguous tasks.
-   Recognition of Humanity deepens accountability When people feel their worth is acknowledged, they’re more likely to take ownership of their work. This expression reinforces dignity, which drives intrinsic motivation and pride in contribution.
-   Clarity reduces rework and accelerates progress Clear, candid, and kind communication ensures expectations are understood. It minimizes confusion, enables better planning, and allows employees to self-correct before errors compound.
-   Adaptability increases effectiveness across differences Adjusting tone and delivery to meet individual contexts helps managers connect across styles and stress responses. This flexibility improves coaching outcomes and team cohesion.

### Why Behavior-Change Science Amplifies These Effects
-   It transforms insight into habit Humane Connection creates the conditions for trust and reflection. Behavior-change science ensures those insights are translated into repeatable actions through cues, reinforcement, and feedback loops.
-   It targets the root, not just the symptom Instead of reacting to performance gaps, behavior-change frameworks help managers identify triggers, beliefs, and environmental factors that shape behavior, leading to more sustainable growth.
-   It builds adaptive capacity By combining emotional resonance with behavioral modeling, managers help employees navigate uncertainty, build new skills, and internalize ownership.
